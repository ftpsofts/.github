## 03 / LATEST PRODUCT — BEBSAI

<div align="center">

<img src="https://cdn.ftpsofts.com/central-brand-assets/01M7738YHAN3NBNKWTMSP3NTBA.webp" width="420" alt="Bebsai — Business for smart people"/>

### Business for smart people.

**An all-in-one e-commerce enablement and intelligent business management platform.**

<a href="https://bebsai.com">
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/EXPLORE-BEBSai-16A34A?style=for-the-badge" alt="Explore Bebsai"/>
</a>

</div>

Bebsai is our latest product, designed to simplify how online businesses are launched, managed, and operated.

Built with Bangladesh's growing digital commerce ecosystem in mind, it brings together storefront management, product operations, order processing, delivery workflows, and business automation.

### Core Capabilities

| Platform Module | Capabilities |
|:---|:---|
| Online Store | Store creation and management |
| Product Management | Product catalogs and inventory operations |
| Order Management | Order collection, processing and tracking |
| Delivery Operations | Courier and fulfillment workflows |
| Business Automation | Centralized operational workflows |
| Marketing Operations | Digital marketing activities |
| AI Content | Intelligent content generation |
| AI Agents | Permission-controlled business automation |

### Agent-Oriented Commerce

Bebsai introduces an agent-oriented approach to e-commerce operations.

Its vision includes specialized AI agents that assist with marketing, content generation, seasonal campaigns, order management, courier placement, and routine digital business operations.

**Merchant-controlled intelligence**

Store owners determine the level of authority granted to their agents. They can authorize automated execution, supervise activities, intervene, interrupt, or override operations whenever necessary.

The store owner retains ultimate authority over the business.
